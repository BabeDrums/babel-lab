## Lab-specific conflict rule

Lab follows Truth by default, but it is allowed to diverge **deliberately** when the experiment itself is testing an alternative.

Before reconciling a difference, classify it:

- **stale divergence** → update Lab to Truth
- **intentional experiment** → preserve the difference and document what it is testing
- **potential upstream improvement** → do not silently overwrite; surface the evidence so Babel can decide whether Truth should change first
- **app/product-local exception** → keep it local and clarify ownership

Update the reconciled Truth commit only after that comparison is complete.

Babel Lab is not Babel World and is not production authority. A World prototype may live here; a real World product earns its own repo later.

## Lab-specific authority rule

> **Lab is evidence, never authority.**

Lab may contain contradictory styles, sizes, buttons, shapes, UIs, modals, apps, and interaction hypotheses simultaneously. There is no canonical "Lab style" for another product to inherit, and no downstream product should reference Lab as a design specification.

Current Truth foundations may be used as a convenient baseline for an experiment, but an experiment may deliberately diverge when the divergence itself is what is being tested.

Before reconciling a difference, classify it:

- **stale divergence** → update the experiment when that is actually desired
- **intentional experiment** → preserve and label it
- **potential upstream improvement** → gather evidence; do not silently change Truth
- **app/product-local exception** → keep it local and clarify ownership

For shell/visual work, promotion is deliberate:

**Lab experiment → physical-device testing → candidate → owning product implementation → owning product physical validation → transferable rules promoted to Babel Truth.**

Update the reconciled Truth commit only after comparison/reconciliation is complete.

Babel Lab is not Babel World, FIRE, or production authority. Real products keep their own repositories and runtime/data.
